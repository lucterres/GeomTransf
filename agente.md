## Nome do Projeto
Geometric transformations

## Objetivo do Projeto
This project is part of a Ablation study. Here We must generate 
one set of 100 masks using simple geometric transformations (e.g.,
rotation, scaling, horizontal/vertical flipping, elastic de-
formation) applied to existing ground-truth masks from
the training set. The set produced here will be compared to another set generated by a trained VAE in other project by sampling from the latent space. 
We will receive the results generated.
The comparation will be made in this project.
The ground-truth masks is on the D:\dataset\tgs-salt\train\masks\*.png

• Hypothesis: We hypothesize that the VAE, by learning
a continuous latent representation of salt geometries,
generates masks with greater diversity and more realistic
geometric characteristics than simple transformations,
which are limited to variations of existing shapes.
• Evaluation: The two mask sets are compared using
quantitative metrics applied directly to the binary masks:
(a) Shape diversity measured by computing pairwise
Intersection-over-Union (IoU) between all masks in
each set—lower average IoU indicates higher diversity;
(b) Geometric realism assessed by comparing statis-
tical distributions of shape descriptors (area, perime-
ter, compactness ratio, eccentricity) between generated
masks and real ground-truth masks using Kolmogorov-
Smirnov test; 
 Superior diversity scores,closer statistical alignment with real masks, and higher
expert ratings (will be made elsewhere) would validate the VAE’s contribution to
generating high-quality geometric priors for synthesis.
